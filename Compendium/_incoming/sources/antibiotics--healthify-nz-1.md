Antibiotics are a group of medicines used to treat bacterial infections. Inappropriate use of antibiotics can cause antibiotic resistance, which is a serious threat to everyone's health. Most antibiotics are available only with a prescription and should be taken only as prescribed to treat bacterial infections that your natural immune system is having difficulty fighting off. Taking antibiotics unnecessarily for viral illnesses may increase the risk of bacteria developing resistance. Different antibiotics affect people differently, but side effects include diarrhoea, nausea, vomiting, and fungal infections. A small number of people might have a severe allergic reaction to some groups of antibiotics. It's important to follow the instructions carefully when taking antibiotics.
