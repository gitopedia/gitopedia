Antibiotics are medications used to treat bacterial infections. They target unique structures in bacteria, either killing them or inhibiting their growth. Antibiotic resistance is a global health challenge caused by misuse and overuse of antibiotics. To preserve their effectiveness, it's crucial to use antibiotics responsibly and complete the full course of medication. Side effects can occur, but they typically resolve after treatment.
