- The Abbasid Caliphate: A Golden Age of Islamic Civilization

- The Abbasid Caliphate was a pivotal period in Islamic history, known for its cultural, scientific, and intellectual achievements.
- It succeeded the Umayyad Caliphate and established a dynasty that saw the Islamic Golden Age flourish.
- Key events included the Battle of Talas and the founding of Baghdad's House of Wisdom.
- Despite its eventual decline after military conflicts and the Mongol invasion, the Abbasid era's contributions to knowledge and culture remain influential.
