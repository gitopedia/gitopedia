- Mars has long been a source of inspiration, imagination and, lately, aspiration for many.
- It has generated a breadth of beliefs and ideas, as well as hope for a bright future there.
- However, the red planet will never offer a fresh start: we would carry with us some of our culture and our politics.
- Our future there would remain connected to Earth’s history, particularly human history.
- In the present chapter, humanity’s relationship to Mars is discussed from a broadly historical, cultural, and ethical perspective, in order to provide a backdrop for the later inquiry of a variety of issues—ethical, societal, and political—related to Mars exploration and settlement.
- Political drivers for establishing a Mars settlement are then discussed.
- The chapter ends on a more practical note: on planetary protection, and the threat posed thereto by human-borne microbes.
- One way or another, terrestrial beings will alter the Martian environment.
- The ethical concerns this raises cannot be left unaddressed.
