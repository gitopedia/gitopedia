Cardiology is a branch of medicine that studies the heart and blood vessels. It involves diagnosing and treating various heart conditions using medicines or procedures that don't involve major surgery. Cardiologists work with other doctors to provide the best care for patients with heart problems or at risk of developing them. They treat common heart conditions such as high blood pressure, heart attacks, heart failure, and rhythm problems. Cardiac surgeons perform operations on the heart and blood vessels, including fixing heart valves. Keeping your heart healthy is crucial by eating a balanced diet, being active, getting enough sleep, managing stress, and avoiding smoking.
