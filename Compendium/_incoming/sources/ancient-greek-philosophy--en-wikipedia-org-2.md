### **2. Key Themes and Figures in Ancient Greek Philosophy**
Use the references to explore these topics:
- **Presocratic Philosophy**: Origins of cosmology, metaphysics, and natural philosophy (e.g., Thales, Heraclitus, Pythagoras).
- **Socratic Dialogue**: The method of questioning and ethics (e.g., Plato’s *Apology*, *Crito*, *Phaedo*).
- **Aristotelianism**: Logic, metaphysics, and ethics (e.g., *Nicomachean Ethics*, *Metaphysics*).
- **Hellenistic Schools**: 
  - **Stoicism**: Zeno of Citium, Seneca, Epictetus.
  - **Epicureanism**: Epicurus, Lucretius.
  - **Skepticism**: Pyrrho, Arcesilaus, Carneades.
- **Platonism and Neoplatonism**: Plato’s theory of Forms, Plotinus, and later Neoplatonists.
- **Myth and Philosophy**: Connections between Greek philosophy and mystery cults (e.g., Orphism, Eleusinian Mysteries).
