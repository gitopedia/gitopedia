The canonical commutation relation is [x^,p^] = iℏ, which implies that the lower bound B for the uncertainty relation σxσp^2 ≥ B is given by:

B = ℏ² / 4.

This result comes from substituting the commutator into the generalized Heisenberg uncertainty principle, leading to the well-known form:

σxσp ≥ ℏ/2.

Thus, the correct lower bound for the uncertainty relation involving x^ and p^2 is:

B = \boxed{\dfrac{\hbar^2}{4}}.
