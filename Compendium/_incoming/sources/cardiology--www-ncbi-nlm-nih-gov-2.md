Cardiology Explained is a comprehensive book that provides clear explanations of cardiovascular physiology, pathophysiology, and various cardiology conditions. The book covers topics such as cardiac arrest, ECG interpretation, hypertension, heart failure, arrhythmias, and more.

The book was written by Euan Ashley and Josef Niebauer and is ideal for hospital doctors, generalists, or even senior medical students who need a cardiology opinion. It's also perfect for anyone interested in understanding cardiology better. The book includes diagrams, case studies, and practical advice to help readers understand complex cardiology concepts.

In summary, Cardiology Explained is an essential resource for healthcare professionals, medical students, and anyone looking to learn more about cardiology.
