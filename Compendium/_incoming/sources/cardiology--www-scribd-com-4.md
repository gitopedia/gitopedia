Cardiology is an overview of various cardiovascular diseases, including definitions, key terms, etiologies, clinical presentations, auscultatory findings, investigations, and treatments. It covers conditions such as hypertension, heart valve diseases, pericarditis, heart failure, congenital defects, and infective endocarditis. Each disease is described with specific symptoms and diagnostic approaches.
