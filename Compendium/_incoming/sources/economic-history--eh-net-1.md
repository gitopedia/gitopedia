- Advisory Board Members and Consulting Editors

- The EH.Net Encyclopedia of Economic and Business History is designed to provide students and laymen with high quality reference articles in the field.
- Articles for the Online Encyclopedia are written by experts, screened by a group of authorities, and carefully edited.
- A distinguished Advisory Board recommends entry topics, assists in the selection of authors, and defines the project’s scope.
