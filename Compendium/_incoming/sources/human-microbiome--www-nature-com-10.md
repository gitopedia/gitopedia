- Milestones in human microbiota research

- The human body is home to trillions of microorganisms that have direct and indirect impacts on health and disease.
- The microbiota that resides on and within us is a diverse community of bacteria, archaea, viruses, and fungi.
- This ‘Milestones in Human Microbiota Research’ provides a historical perspective of the breakthroughs in the field as well as future research directions.
