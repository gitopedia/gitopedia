# Dark Energy
- Dark energy is a mysterious effect that causes the expansion of the universe to accelerate.
- Fermilab’s dark energy experiments use precision measurements from large cosmic surveys to study the nature of matter, space-time, and their interactions on the largest scales.

# Dark Energy Experiments
- The Dark Energy Survey (DES) uses a large CCD camera on the 4m Blanco telescope at CTIO in Chile.
- The Rubin Observatory Legacy Survey of Space and Time (LSST) will conduct a similar survey but on a much larger scale with a new telescope in Chile.
- The Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) will deploy an instrument on the Mayall telescope at Kitt Peak in Arizona to make precision spectroscopic measurements of distant objects.

# Rubin Observatory LSST
- The Rubin Observatory LSST is a large-scale survey that aims to provide detailed understanding of dark energy by studying the effects of dark energy further back in time.
