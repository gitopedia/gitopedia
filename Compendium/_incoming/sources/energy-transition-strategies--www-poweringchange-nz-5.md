# Energy Transition Strategies

- The Energy Transition Framework

- "The way we power New Zealand is changing rapidly. That means our energy system must change too. Given what's at stake, it's crucial the sector comes together to tackle the key challenges." – Energy Transition Framework Chair Nigel Barbour

- New Zealand's Energy Sector leaders have come together to launch the Energy Transition Framework.
- They are a diverse group of industry participants, from generators to retailers, lines and distribution companies, and independent consumer and advisory representatives.The top priorities are clear: delivering secure, affordable, and abundant energy for New Zealand homes and businesses.
- The Framework represents a landmark commitment to working collectively – and urgently – to ensure that, as our energy system transforms, it remains secure, reliable, affordable, and supportive of a high-growth economy.Read the Energy Transition Framework document.

- The Framework Work Programme

- Under the Framework, participants will come together to work on key priorities for the energy system.
- These priorities and the work completed under the Framework will change over time.
- Below are the current workstreams underway – which include providing more information about the transition on this Powering Change website.

- Exploring the future of the gas system

- Workstream Description We’re taking a close look at how New Zealand’s gas system is working now, and what might need to change in the future. This includes looking at how households and businesses that need gas can get it.

- Why It’s Important Gas is used in lots of different ways across New Zealand and currently not everyone who needs it can get it. By understanding the whole picture, we can make smart choices that work best for people and businesses across the country.

- A gas working group has reviewed what we know about the gas system and produced this report identifying key information. This summary of initial findings was shared with energy leaders in August.

- Next StepsThe group are looking at ways to complete the analysis that the report suggests should happen.

- How finance can help businesses switch to electricity

- Workstream Description This work is focused on helping businesses—especially those that use a lot of energy—make the switch from other energy sources to electricity.

- Why It's ImportantIf large businesses can switch from fossil fuels to electricity it can help strengthen the overall energy system in ways that benefit everyone. Supporting these businesses to make this change also makes a big difference in helping New Zealand lower its overall emissions.

- We’ve started by collecting information on the biggest challenges businesses face, especially around how to pay for the switch. A summary of what we've learned so far can be found in this interim report, which also identifies the plan for designing solutions in the next stage.

- Next StepsNext, we are working across four solution workstreams, with experts from finance and energy. We expect to provide an update in November. After that, we'll figure out how these solutions can be rolled out in the real world.

- Powering Change – sharing clear information about the transition

- Workstream DescriptionPowering Change is a public information programme that helps people better understand how the energy system is changing and what it means for them.

- Why It's ImportantBy sharing information about how the transition is progressing we can hold ourselves to account and also help New Zealanders make smart decisions about their energy use.

- What We're DoingWe’re refreshing the Powering Change website to include: an overview of the Framework and what it’s working on; easy-to-understand updates on progress; and a regular report showing how we’re tracking toward our goals.

- Next StepsNext steps include new metrics and measures reporting on the Powering Change website in September, followed by the public campaign in November.
