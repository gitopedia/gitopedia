# It All Started With Cepheids
- American astronomer Henrietta Swan Leavitt discovered the period-luminosity relationship in Cepheid variables, enabling measurement of distances to stars and galaxies.
- Vesto Slipher used a spectrograph to observe redshift in distant galaxies, indicating their movement away from Earth.

# Discovering an Expanding Universe
- Alexander Friedmann published a paper in 1922 suggesting the universe could be expanding based on Einstein's theory of general relativity.
- Georges Lemaître independently proposed that the universe is expanding using Einstein's equations in 1927.
- Edwin Hubble and Milton Humason confirmed the universe's expansion in 1929 by measuring redshift and distance of galaxies, establishing Hubble's Law.

# Expansion is Speeding Up, Supernovae Show
- In 1998, astronomers Adam Riess, Saul Perlmutter, and Brian Schmidt observed Type 1a supernovae appearing dimmer than expected, indicating faster expansion.
- This discovery led to the conclusion that the universe's expansion is accelerating.

# What Exactly is Dark Energy?
- Dark energy is a mysterious force causing the universe's accelerated expansion, comprising about 68.3-70% of the universe.
- Theorized explanations include vacuum energy (cosmological constant), quintessence (a varying energy field), and cosmic defects like cosmic strings.

# A Flaw in General Relativity
- Some scientists propose modifying general relativity to explain expansion without dark energy, such as through unimodular gravity.
- Dark energy is distinct from dark matter, which makes up about 85% of the universe's matter.

# NASA's Role in Studying Dark Energy
- **Euclid Mission (launched 2023):** Maps universe structure to study dark energy effects over time.
- **Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope (launching May 2027):** Creates a 3D dark matter map, observes Type Ia supernovae, with a field of view 100 times Hubble's.
- **Vera C. Rubin Observatory (operational 2025):** Ground-based observatory supporting dark energy research.
- **James Webb Space Telescope (launched 2021):** Contributes to dark energy studies through infrared observations.
- **SPHEREx Mission (launching April 2025):** Surveys galaxies in near-infrared light, aiding dark energy understanding.
- **Dark Energy Explorers:** Citizen science project enabling public participation in dark energy research.

# Collaborative Efforts
- NASA collaborates with the European Space Agency (ESA) and other international partners on missions like Euclid and Roman to advance cosmology studies.
