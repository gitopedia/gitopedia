The history of antibiotics dates back to the late 19th century with the observation of antibacterial chemicals by scientists. Paul Ehrlich discovered arsphenamine, while Selman Waksman discovered penicillin. Alexander Fleming accidentally discovered penicillin in 1928 and it was later used to treat human wounds during World War II.
