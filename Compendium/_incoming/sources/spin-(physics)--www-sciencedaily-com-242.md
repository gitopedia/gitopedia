# Spin (Physics)
- Spin refers to the angular momentum intrinsic to a body in physics.
- It is distinct from orbital angular momentum, which arises from the motion of an object's center of mass about an external point.
- Spin is a fundamental concept in quantum mechanics and relates to the properties of particles such as electrons.
