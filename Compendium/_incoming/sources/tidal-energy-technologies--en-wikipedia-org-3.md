### **Key Topics Covered in the Text**
1. **Tidal Energy Overview**  
   - **Definition**: Harnessing energy from tidal movements (e.g., tidal streams, barrages, lagoons).  
   - **History**: Early projects like the **Annapolis Tidal Power Pilot (1980)** and **Severn Barrage** (planned but not completed).  
   - **Technology**:  
     - **Tidal Stream Generators**: Underwater turbines (e.g., **European Marine Energy Centre**).  
     - **Tidal Barrages**: Dams that capture energy from tidal differences (e.g., **Sihwa Lake Tidal Plant** in South Korea).  
     - **Tidal Lagoons**: Enclosed areas that store and release water (e.g., **MeyGen** in Scotland).  

2. **Resources and Databases**
