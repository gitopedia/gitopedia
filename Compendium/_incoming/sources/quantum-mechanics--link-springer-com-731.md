# Book Description
- Second edition builds upon topics covered in the first edition.
- New chapters include Hilbert spaces and unbounded operators, minimum uncertainty states, path integrals in general coordinates, Fock spaces, second quantization, relativistic particle states, and quantum fields.
- Historical insights: Pre-history of matrix mechanics and Pauli's proof of the H-atom spectrum using O(4) symmetry.
- Introduces Bell's inequality and non-locality in quantum mechanics.

# Author Information
