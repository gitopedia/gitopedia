### Extracted Facts:

1. **Dirac Equation Overview**:
   - The Dirac equation describes the quantum states of fermions.
   - It was formulated by Paul Dirac.

2. **Key Features and Concepts**:
   - Incorporates spin-½ particles.
   - Predicts negative energy solutions, leading to the concept of antimatter.
   - Introduces gamma matrices to describe relativistic properties and ensure Lorentz invariance.
   - Spinors (ψ) have four components.

3. **Predictions and Applications**:
   - Predicted the existence of positrons (antimatter).
   - Explains the magnetic moment of electrons, including the anomalous magnetic moment.
   - Used in Quantum Electrodynamics (QED), relativistic quantum mechanics, and various areas of particle physics.
   - Applied to describe fermions like electrons, muons, and quarks.

4. **Alternative Formulations**:
   - Foldy-Wouthuysen Transformation: Simplifies non-relativistic interpretations.
   - Weyl (Chiral) Representation: Emphasizes chirality in particles.
   - Majorana Representation: Describes Majorana fermions.

5. **Applications in Physics**:
   - Hawking Radiation: Predicts particle-antiparticle pairs near black holes.
   - Condensed Matter Physics: Models materials like graphene with Dirac fermions.
   - Neutrino Oscillations: Extended to include neutrino mass terms.

6. **Comparison with Schrödinger Equation**:
   - Relativistic and incorporates spin, unlike the non-relativistic Schrödinger equation.
