## Overview

Quantum entanglement is a phenomenon in quantum physics where particles originating from the same source remain connected even after being separated by vast distances in time and space. When particles are entangled, they share a unified quantum state that persists regardless of their spatial separation. This means that any change or measurement performed on one particle instantaneously influences its entangled counterpart(s), no matter how far apart they may be.
