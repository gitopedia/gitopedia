    spans five primary sources: solar, wind, hydropower, geothermal, and bioenergy. Solar energy has roots dating back to ancient times, with Greeks and Romans utilizing burning mirrors for lighting torches and later advancements leading to efficient solar cell technology. Wind energy has been used mechanically since the Middle Ages, while modern electrical applications emerged in the 19th century. Hydropower has been harnessed since antiquity but saw significant development during the late 1800s, while geothermal energy has been utilized for heating and cooling since the Paleolithic period. Bioenergy, derived from organic materials, has been used for lighting and heat generation throughout history. Today, renewable energy sources play a crucial role in meeting ESG goals and reducing greenhouse gas emissions.}
