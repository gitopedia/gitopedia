- Foundations of Neuroeconomics: History and Methods

- Neuroeconomics is an interdisciplinary field integrating neuroscience, psychology, and economics to understand how people make decisions.
- Departing from traditional economic models, which infer preferences solely from observed choices, neuroeconomics seeks to elucidate how the brain computes value, updates beliefs, manages uncertainty, and incorporates social information.
- This section introduces foundational studies that defined the early trajectory of the field, establishing core concepts, questions, and methodologies.
- It also presents key empirical and computational methods—such as neuroimaging, reinforcement learning models, lesion studies, and behavioral economic tasks—that have become standard approaches in neuroeconomics.
- Collectively, these contributions provide an essential starting point for understanding both the historical context and future directions of neuroeconomic research.
