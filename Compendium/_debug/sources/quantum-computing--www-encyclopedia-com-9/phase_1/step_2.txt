maintaining qubit stability.\n- Error correction and fault tolerance are critical areas of research for scalable quantum systems.\n- Current quantum computers are limited to small numbers of qubits and require extreme cooling and isolation.\n\n## Applications and Limitations\n- Potential applications include cryptography, drug discovery, and optimization problems.\n- Quantum computing is not a
