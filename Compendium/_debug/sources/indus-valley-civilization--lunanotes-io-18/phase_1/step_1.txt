### **Key Highlights of the Indus Valley Civilization**  
1. **Geography & Timeline**:  
   - Emerged around 2600 BCE in present-day India and Pakistan.  
   - Spread across regions like Punjab, Sindh, and Gujarat.  

2. **Urban Planning & Architecture**:  
   - Grid-based city layouts (e.g., Mohenjo-Daro, Harappa).  
   - Advanced drainage systems, public baths (like the Great Bath), and fortified citadels.  
   - Evidence of planned streets, multi-story buildings, and standardized weights/measurements.  

3. **Economy & Trade**:  
   - Thriving trade with Mesopotamia (e.g., seals, pottery, and Indus script).  
   - Use of standardized weights, measures, and currency-like systems.  

4. **Artifacts & Culture**:  
   - Terracotta figurines, seals (with animal motifs), and intricate pottery.  
   - Evidence of a complex society with possible social stratification.  

5. **Decline & Legacy**:  
   - Theories about decline include environmental changes, shifts in trade routes, or internal factors.  
   - The IVC’s sophistication challenges colonial notions of Western superiority.  

### **Archaeological Sites**  
- **Harappa & Mohenjo-Daro**: Iconic cities with grid layouts and drainage.  
- **Dholavira**: Known for water management systems.  
- **Lothal**: A coastal site with dockyards, suggesting maritime trade.  

### **Connection to Modern Studies**  
- The IVC’s urban planning parallels modern city designs.  
- Its decline remains a topic of debate, with ongoing research into climate, socio-political factors, and external influences.
