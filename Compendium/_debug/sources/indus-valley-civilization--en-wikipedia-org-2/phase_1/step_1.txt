### **1. Indus Valley Civilization Overview**
The **Indus Valley Civilization** (c. 3300–1300 BCE) was one of the world's earliest urban civilizations, centered in present-day Pakistan and northwest India. Key features include:
- **Urban Planning**: Grid-pattern cities (e.g., Mohenjo-daro, Harappa) with advanced drainage systems.
- **Sanitation**: Covered drains, public baths (e.g., the **Great Bath** of Mohenjo-daro), and waste disposal systems.
- **Architecture**: Multi-story buildings, standardized bricks, and sophisticated water management.
- **Cultural Artifacts**: Seals with animal motifs, pottery (e.g., **Ochre Coloured Pottery**), and trade networks with Mesopotamia.
- **Mystery**: Lack of deciphered script and unclear governance structures.
