### **Key Facts About the Indus Valley Civilization**  
1. **Urban Planning & Sanitation**  
   - Cities like **Mohenjo-Daro** and **Dholavira** had **grid layouts**, **drainage systems**, and **public baths**, showcasing advanced urban planning.  
   - **Dholavira** featured the **world’s earliest known signboard**, indicating early literacy or symbolic communication.  

2. **Technological Innovations**  
   - **Metallurgy**: Bronze and copper tools, gold jewelry (e.g., 0.25 mm gold beads), and advanced casting techniques.  
   - **Dentistry**: Evidence of **dental drilling** (5500–7000 BCE) in **Mehrgarh**, suggesting proto-dentistry.  
   - **Water Management**: Reservoirs, dams, and sophisticated irrigation systems.  

3. **Social Structure**  
   - **No depictions of rulers**: No evidence of kings or warfare, suggesting a **peaceful, egalitarian society**.

4. **Resilience & Rebuilding**  
   - **Mohenjo-Daro** was rebuilt **9 times**, adhering to the same grid pattern despite floods and sedimentation.  

5. **Cultural & Economic Traits**  
   - **Trade Networks**: Exchanged goods like cotton, beads, and pottery with Mesopotamia and beyond.  
   - **Standardized Weights & Measures**: Suggests a centralized economic system.
