Solar energy is a type of renewable energy that is generated by converting sunlight into electricity. Solar panels are the most common way to harness solar energy and convert it into usable electricity for homes and businesses. By installing solar panels on your roof or property, you can generate clean, renewable energy that can be used to power your home or business, reducing your reliance on fossil fuels and lowering your energy costs.

In addition to solar panels, there are other ways to harness solar energy such as solar thermal systems which use sunlight to heat water for hot water heating or space heating. Solar PV (photovoltaic) technology is the most common way of generating electricity from sunlight and has become increasingly affordable in recent years due to advancements in technology and economies of scale.

Solar energy offers many benefits, including reducing your carbon footprint, saving money on energy bills, increasing the value of your property, and contributing to a more sustainable future.
