CRISPR gene editing has taken the scientific world by storm, offering unprecedented possibilities in genetic research and medicine. But what exactly is CRISPR? CRISPR stands for Clustered Regularly Interspaced Short Palindromic Repeats, a technology that allows scientists to make precise changes to DNA. Imagine being able to cut out a faulty gene and replace it with a healthy one—CRISPR makes this possible. This breakthrough has the potential to cure genetic disorders, improve crop resilience, and even combat diseases like cancer. However, with great power comes great responsibility, and ethical concerns loom large. What are the most intriguing facts about CRISPR gene editing?
