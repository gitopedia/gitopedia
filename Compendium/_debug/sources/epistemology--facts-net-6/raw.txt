Written by Catherin Meadows

Epistemology dives deep into questions like, "What is knowledge?" and "How do we acquire it?" This field examines the difference between justified belief and mere opinion. It also explores the sources of knowledge, such as perception, reason, memory, and testimony. Understanding epistemology helps us navigate complex ideas and make sense of the world.
