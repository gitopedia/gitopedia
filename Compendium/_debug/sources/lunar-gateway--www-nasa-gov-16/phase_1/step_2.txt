- International teams of astronauts will explore the scientific mysteries of deep space with Gateway, humanity’s first space station around the Moon.

- Humanity's next science platform

- Gateway is central to the NASA-led Artemis missions to return to the Moon for scientific discovery and chart a path for the first human missions to Mars and beyond.
- The small space station will be a multi-purpose outpost supporting lunar surface missions, science in lunar orbit, and human exploration further into the cosmos.
- NASA is working with commercial and international partners to build humanity's Gateway.

- International Partners

- Gateway's HALO Arrives Stateside

- The arrival of HALO (Habitation and Logistics Outpost) in the U.S. marked a major milestone in the development of Gateway—humanity’s first space station to orbit the Moon.
- As the first long-term home for astronauts in lunar orbit, HALO will support Gateway’s role advancing NASA’s Artemis campaign and preparing the agency and its partners for the first crewed missions to Mars.
