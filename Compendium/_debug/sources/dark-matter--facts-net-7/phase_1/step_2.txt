- 39 Facts About Darkmatter

- Written by Esta Randolph

- Published: 08 Mar 2025

- Dark matter is one of the universe's biggest mysteries.
- Scientists believe it makes up about 27% of the universe, yet we can't see or touch it.
- So, what exactly is dark matter?
- Dark matter is a type of matter that doesn't emit light or energy, making it invisible.
- We only know it exists because of its gravitational effects on visible matter, like stars and galaxies.
- Imagine a hidden force holding the universe together!
- This blog post will dive into 39 fascinating facts about dark matter, shedding light on what we know and what remains a mystery.
- Get ready to explore the unseen!
