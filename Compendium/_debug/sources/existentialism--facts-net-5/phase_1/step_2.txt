- 37 Facts About Existentialism

- Written by Caterina Natale

- Published: 04 May 2025

- Existentialism is a philosophical movement that explores the meaning of existence, freedom, and choice.
- But what exactly is existentialism?
- In essence, existentialism focuses on individual experience, emphasizing personal responsibility and the search for meaning in a seemingly indifferent universe.
- This philosophy emerged in the 19th and 20th centuries, with thinkers like Søren Kierkegaard, Friedrich Nietzsche, Jean-Paul Sartre, and Simone de Beauvoir leading the charge.
- Existentialism challenges us to confront the absurdity of life and make authentic choices despite uncertainty.
- From literature to psychology, its influence stretches far and wide, shaping how we understand human nature and our place in the world.
